livelooper

designed by groovex · BeatTactics

This installer puts livelooper on your Mac:

  • livelooper app (standalone) in /Applications
  • VST3 plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit plug-in in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

Under „Customize“ you can leave out parts. Close your DAWs before installing.

The ZIP download and the manual installation keep working as an alternative.
