# Drum samples - sources and licenses

Every drum sample in livelooper comes from a source released under **CC0 1.0** or into the **public domain**. The files here were converted to mono 44.1 kHz / 24-bit FLAC, trimmed, faded and normalised by `tools/samples/build_drum_samples.py` (the Lo-Fi kit is additionally reduced to 12 bit / 26 kHz). Built-in kit names describe a sound style; they do not imply endorsement by any manufacturer.
