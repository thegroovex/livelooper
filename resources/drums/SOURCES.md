# Drum samples - sources and licenses

Every drum sample in livelooper comes from a source released under **CC0 1.0** or into the **public domain**, or was synthesised for livelooper by `tools/samples/synth_kits.py` (oscillators and noise, no recordings; released under CC0 1.0). The files here were converted to mono 44.1 kHz FLAC (24 bit; the v1.6 style kits 16 bit), trimmed, faded and normalised by `tools/samples/build_drum_samples.py` (the Lo-Fi kit is additionally reduced to 12 bit / 26 kHz). Built-in kit names describe a sound style; they do not imply endorsement by any manufacturer.

v1.6 kits: House, Techno, Boom Bap, Trap, Breaks and Disco are fully self-synthesised; Rock and Afro Latin use VCSL recordings (CC0) plus self-synthesised kick layers; the Studio kick and snare got extra takes derived from their VCSL recordings.
