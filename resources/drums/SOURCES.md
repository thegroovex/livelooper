# Drum samples - sources and licenses

Every drum sample in livelooper is a real recording from a source released under **CC0 1.0** or into the **public domain**. The files here were converted to mono 44.1 kHz FLAC (24 bit; the v1.6 style kits 16 bit), trimmed, faded and normalised by `tools/samples/build_drum_samples.py`; sources recorded with several microphones were mixed down to mono, some takes were derived from a recording (pitch / decay / transient variant, marked as such), the Lo-Fi kit is reduced to 12 bit / 26 kHz and the Boom Bap kit to 12 bit / 30 kHz. Built-in kit names describe a sound style; they do not imply endorsement by any manufacturer.

v1.6 style kits: House and Techno use freesound.org CC0 samples (as shipped with Sonic Pi) plus real hand claps; Trap uses the CC0 TR-808 set by Michael Fischer, freesound CC0 hats and real claps; Boom Bap, Breaks and Disco use the CC0 acoustic kit Virtuosity Drums; Rock uses Big Rusty Drums; Disco and Afro Latin also use Swirly Drums and Frankensnare; Afro Latin is real hand percussion (cajon, bongo, claves, shaker, tambourine, cowbell, cabasa, conga). No sound is synthesised.
